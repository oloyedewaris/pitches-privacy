    INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

    This Privacy Policy explains how Plinths and Pitches Investment Limited ("Plinths and Pitches", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores, discloses, transfers, protects and otherwise processes personal data.

    It applies when you visit or use our website, mobile application, customer portal or related digital services; make an enquiry; create an account; request or purchase a property; make or receive a payment; visit our office or project locations; communicate with us; participate in a promotion or event; apply for a role; supply services to us; or otherwise interact with Plinths and Pitches.

    It applies to customers, prospective customers, website and app users, property visitors, authorised representatives, guarantors or co-purchasers, beneficiaries, vendors, contractors, professional contacts and job applicants. Employees and workers may receive a separate internal privacy notice.

    This Policy is designed to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, applicable regulations and directives issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, and other applicable Nigerian laws. Where another law requires additional or different processing, that law will apply.

    DATA RETENTION

    Plinths and Pitches keeps personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to complete and administer property transactions, maintain title and allocation records, meet statutory recordkeeping duties, resolve complaints, prevent fraud and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

    Retention decisions consider the nature and sensitivity of the data, the transaction or relationship, the risk of harm, contractual requirements, applicable limitation periods, legal and regulatory obligations, and whether the purpose can be achieved through anonymisation.

    Property ownership, allocation, title, payment and compliance records may require longer retention than ordinary enquiries because they evidence continuing rights, obligations and regulatory history. When data is no longer required, Plinths and Pitches will securely delete, destroy or irreversibly anonymise it, unless lawful archival retention applies.

    Schedule 1 provides the retention framework. Operational retention periods may be further specified in Plinths and Pitches' internal retention schedule or a transaction-specific notice.

    SECURITY AND PERSONAL-DATA BREACHES

    Plinths and Pitches applies technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, alteration, disclosure or access. Measures are selected according to the nature of the data, processing context, available technology, implementation cost and risk to individuals.

    access controls, role-based permissions and account-authentication measures;

    encryption in transit and, where appropriate, at rest;

    logging, monitoring, malware protection, backups and recovery procedures;

    secure development, configuration, patching and vulnerability management;

    confidentiality obligations, staff awareness and incident-response procedures;

    service-provider assessment and contractual security requirements; and

    periodic reviews, testing and impact assessments where the processing is likely to present high risk.

    No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Plinths and Pitches does not guarantee absolute security, but will maintain measures appropriate to the risk and respond to suspected incidents in accordance with law.

    Where a personal-data breach occurs, Plinths and Pitches will investigate, contain and remediate it; document the incident; notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where required; and notify affected individuals where the law requires or where notice is otherwise appropriate.

    You are responsible for keeping account credentials confidential, using a strong unique password, protecting your device and promptly reporting suspected account compromise or unauthorised activity.
